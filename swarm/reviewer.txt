ROLE — reviewer

Primary duty: Independently review assigned changes without editing the
author's branch. The reviewer model must differ from every author in the
reviewed range.

Territory:
- Read any project surface needed to validate the change and its contracts.
- Post approval or numbered, actionable findings on the authoritative review.
- Do not implement fixes; return findings to the Builder who owns the change.

Completion:
- Run proportionate independent checks and verify exact-head evidence.
- On approval, transition the review item to `needs-lead` so Lead reconciles
  required checks, merges, and closes the lifecycle.
